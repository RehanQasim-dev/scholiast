
## [2026-08-25] orchestrator — root-caused the "black screen" FORTIFY crash (NOT app code)
- **Symptom:** ~seconds-to-minutes after launch under the waydroid-inspect harness, process SIGABRTs (`FORTIFY: pthread_mutex_lock called on a destroyed mutex`), WebView goes black, a11y tree collapses to an empty WebView shell.
- **Evidence:** crash buffer backtrace (pid 112361, tid `hwuiTask1`): `pthread_cond_wait → HandleUsingDestroyedMutex → libhwui.so android::uirenderer::CommonPool::$_0`. Our Rust/JS frames absent entirely. Crash reproduced once on a scrcpy virtual display (display 7) ~80s into a fresh process with no interaction; NOT reproduced on the default display across multiple boots + a 2-minute soak (alive, zero new crash entries).
- **Diagnosis:** Android 15 (Lineage 22) HWUI CommonPool teardown race triggered by the harness's virtual-display attach/detach around force-stop+relaunch cycles. Framework bug, not reachable from app code. Same category as finding #2 (singleTask multi-display redirect): both are test-harness artifacts, not user-facing defects.
- **Harness rules going forward (SQA briefs must state):** force-stop the app before launching on a DIFFERENT display; treat libhwui CommonPool FORTIFY aborts on secondary displays as S3-environment findings with the backtrace cited, never as app crashes; verify "crash" reports against `-b crash` backtraces before filing S1s.
